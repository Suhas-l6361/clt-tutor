Frequently Asked Questions

CLAT 2027 — Common Law Admission Test

When will CLAT 2027 registration start and end?

The registration window for CLAT 2027 opened on 3 August 2026 and closes on 31 October 2026. Candidates should complete their applications well before the deadline to avoid last-minute issues.

What is the CLAT 2027 exam date?

CLAT 2027 will be held on 6 December 2026 (Sunday) in offline (pen-and-paper) mode. The exam timings will be printed on the admit card (the previous exam was held from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM).

Who conducts the CLAT exam?

The Consortium of National Law Universities (CNLU) is responsible for organizing the Common Law Admission Test (CLAT) for both undergraduate (UG) and postgraduate (PG) law programs.

How many NLUs accept CLAT scores?

More than 24 National Law Universities (NLUs) across India accept CLAT scores for admission to their integrated law (BA LLB) and postgraduate (LLM) programs. The current list of participating NLUs is published on the Consortium website. Many private universities and public sector organizations also consider CLAT scores for admissions and recruitment.

What is the mode of the exam?

CLAT 2027 will be conducted in offline mode (pen-and-paper test). Candidates must mark their answers on an OMR sheet.

What is the eligibility for CLAT UG 2027?

Students must have passed Class 12 or equivalent with at least 45% marks (40% for SC/ST). Those appearing for their Class 12 exams in 2027 are also eligible to apply.

Is there an age limit for CLAT?

No. There is no upper age limit for CLAT UG or PG. Candidates of any age who meet the educational requirement can apply.

What subjects are covered in CLAT UG?

The CLAT UG exam tests candidates in English Language, Current Affairs including General Knowledge, Legal Reasoning, Logical Reasoning, and Quantitative Techniques.

What is the approximate section-wise weightage in CLAT UG?
  • English Language: about 20–25%
  • Current Affairs including General Knowledge: about 25–30%
  • Legal Reasoning: about 25–30%
  • Logical Reasoning: about 20–25%
  • Quantitative Techniques: about 10–15%
What is the exam pattern and marking scheme?

The paper consists of 120 multiple-choice questions (MCQs) to be completed in 2 hours. Each correct answer carries 1 mark, while 0.25 marks are deducted for every wrong response. The Consortium has confirmed there is no change in the syllabus or exam pattern for CLAT 2027.

Is prior knowledge of law required for CLAT UG?

No. The Legal Reasoning section is passage-based. It tests your ability to apply the principles given in a passage to a set of facts, not memorized legal knowledge.

Are there sectional cut-offs in CLAT?

No. Merit lists are prepared on the basis of the total score across all sections.

How are ties broken in the CLAT merit list?

If two candidates have the same total score, the candidate with the higher score in Legal Reasoning is ranked higher. If they are still tied, the older candidate is ranked higher, and after that the tie is broken by a draw of lots.

How do I apply for CLAT?

Applications are accepted online only through the Consortium website. Register with your mobile number and email, fill in your details, upload your photograph and signature, choose your exam centre preferences, and pay the application fee online.

What is the CLAT application fee?

For CLAT 2026, the application fee was ₹4,000 for General/OBC/PwD candidates and ₹3,500 for SC/ST/BPL candidates. The fee is non-refundable. Please check the CLAT 2027 brochure for the current fee.

Can I correct my application after submitting it?

Yes. The Consortium usually opens a correction window during which candidates can edit certain details, such as exam centre preferences. Changes are not allowed after this window closes.

When will the admit card be released?

The CLAT 2027 admit card is expected about two weeks before the exam, on the official website. Candidates should regularly check consortiumofnlus.ac.in for updates.

Will an answer key be released?

Yes. After the exam, the Consortium publishes a provisional answer key. Candidates can raise objections for a fee within the specified window. The final answer key and results are released after the objections have been reviewed.

When will the CLAT 2027 result be declared?

Results are usually declared within about two weeks of the exam. For reference, the CLAT 2026 result was announced on 16 December 2025.

How does CLAT counselling work?

Admission to NLUs is through centralised online counselling based on CLAT rank and the candidate’s NLU preferences. CLAT 2026 counselling was conducted in five rounds. Counselling registration is separate from exam registration.

What fees are charged during counselling?

For CLAT 2026, candidates paid a counselling registration fee of ₹30,000 (₹20,000 for reserved categories) and a non-refundable confirmation fee of ₹20,000 once a seat was allotted. Both are adjusted against the university fee.

Do NLUs have domicile or state reservation?

Yes. Many NLUs reserve a share of seats for candidates domiciled in their home state, in addition to SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD reservations. Policies vary by university, so check each NLU’s brochure before filling in your preferences.

What is the eligibility for CLAT PG (LLM)?

Candidates need an LLB or equivalent degree with at least 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/PwD). Students appearing for their final-year LLB exams are also eligible.

What is the CLAT PG exam pattern?

CLAT PG has 120 MCQs to be answered in 2 hours. Questions are based on passages from areas such as Constitutional Law, Jurisprudence, Criminal Law, Contract Law, and Torts.

How can I stay updated about CLAT announcements?

All official notifications, updates, and results are published on the Consortium of NLUs official website — consortiumofnlus.ac.in.

Note: Fee and counselling details are based on CLAT 2026 and may change. Always confirm with the official CLAT 2027 brochure.
